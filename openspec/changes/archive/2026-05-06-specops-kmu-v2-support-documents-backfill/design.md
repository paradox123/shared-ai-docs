# Design

The selected files are durable support documents for the current KI-fuer-KMU v2 planning surface:

1. Application Flow documents the product/user path.
2. Slice Plan documents delivery slicing and coverage.
3. S0 Freeze/Legacy Quarantine records the source-of-truth and legacy-source decision.

They are modeled as `type: document` entities and related to the existing v2 master and child-spec entities rather than promoted to primary specs.

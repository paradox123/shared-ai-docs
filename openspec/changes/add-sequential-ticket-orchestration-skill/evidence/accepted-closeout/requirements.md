## Standards ownership for structural review

- **DRY:** single policy owner, caller/template duplication, coherent sharing in local Python helper and tests; distinguish historical reports/spec requirements from copied executable instructions.
- **SOLID:** responsibilities between implementation, acceptance, verification, review, archive and delivery; authority/target boundaries; helper read/write contracts; retained local repository standards; preservation of unrelated dirty work/vendor ownership.
- **KISS:** clarity, unnecessary branches/abstractions, concise progressive disclosure, truthful examples/references; remaining non-automated standards from shared, NCG, probare and KI AGENTS.
- **Automated/direct:** OpenSpec strict, whitespace, helper public CLI behavior and negative boundaries, active-link and identity checks; actual invocation timing and evidence reuse in the example.
